# Register rules

The SANER 2026 reviews rejected the writing, not the idea. All three reviews
raised terms used without definition and claims stronger than the evidence. New
numbers will not repair this. Every draft section is checked against the rules
below before it is considered done.

1. **Descriptive verbs.** The study *reports*, *measures*, *classifies*,
   *compares*. It does not *deconstruct*, *reveal*, *demonstrate the impact of*.
2. **Define every term before first use.** Including: *valid*, *extension*,
   *primary language*, *Mono-fix*, *Poly-fix*, *Other-fix*, *feasibility*,
   *failure mode*, *retrieval recall*, *Top-K*, *MRR*, *MAP*, *classical IR*,
   *language-agnostic*, *parsing / structure-dependent technique*, *cold start*,
   *lexical gap*, *FAISS*. The glossary (`GLOSSARY.md`) is the master list.
3. **No adjective attached to a number.** Not "a substantial 47%", not "only 12
   projects". State the number; let the reader judge.
4. **No "first", no "comprehensive".** No claims about what results will show
   before they exist.
5. **One term, used consistently.** "Technique" throughout (not alternating with
   "model", "tool", "approach").
6. **Exploratory, not confirmatory.** Research questions are descriptive.
   Where a prediction is stated it is labelled a prediction. A statistical test
   is run only where a specific comparison calls for one; "statistically
   significant" appears only next to that test.
7. **A null result is reported as a result**, not reframed.
8. **Formal notation only where it is used.** No equations that are never
   referenced again; no set-difference written as division.
